Authentic Dhaba-Style Paneer Butter Masala

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins 🔥 Cook: 25 mins 👥 Serves: 4 🌶️ Spice: Medium
🔍
🥘

Rich, creamy tomato gravy with slow-roasted aromatic spices & velvety cottage cheese.

🌿 Essential Ingredients

  • 400g Fresh Malai Paneer (cubed)
  • 4 large Vine-ripened Tomatoes (pureed)
  • 2 tbsp Pure Desi Ghee & Salted Butter
  • 12-15 Whole Cashews (soaked & ground)
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tbsp Kasuri Methi (crushed)
  • 2 Green Cardamoms & 1 Bay Leaf
  • 1/2 cup Fresh Dairy Cream

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Method

  1. Aromatic Base: Heat 2 tbsp ghee in a heavy-bottomed kadai. Crackle whole green cardamoms and bay leaf until fragrant.
  2. Tomato Gravy: Add the fresh tomato puree, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, and Kashmiri red chilli. Cook on medium flame until oil separates (approx. 8-10 mins).
  3. Cashew Silk: Whisk in the smooth cashew paste with 1/2 cup warm water. Simmer on low heat until velvety and thick.
  4. Paneer Addition: Gently fold in the fresh paneer cubes. Simmer for 3 minutes without overcooking to keep paneer tender.
  5. Finishing Touch: Finish with crushed kasuri methi, garam masala, and fresh cream. Serve hot with garlic butter naan or jeera rice.

💡 Chef's Secret Tip

Always soak store-bought paneer in warm salted water for 10 minutes before adding to the gravy to keep it exceptionally soft and succulent.
